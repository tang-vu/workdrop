# Crypto World's Fair submission rules and track notes

Checked **2026-09-23** against the linked official organizer and sponsor pages. This is a planning record, not a statement that WORKDROP has qualified or submitted. Times below use Vietnam time (ICT, UTC+7). Recheck the live pages before submission because the [official rules](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf) permit changes.

## Main Colosseum event

- The contest runs from **2026-09-14 06:00 Pacific** through **2026-10-12 23:59 Pacific**. On October 12 Pacific time is PDT (UTC−7), so the closing time is **2026-10-13 13:59 ICT**. Every member must register; the leader submits the project by the deadline. Each entrant may be on one team and each team may submit one project. [Official rules, sections 5–7](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)
- Eligibility includes the age of majority in the entrant's country or 18, whichever is older, plus geographic, sanctions, employer and legal restrictions in section 3. Individual eligibility has not been checked for this team. [Official rules, section 3](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)
- Work from before the contest may be used, but only work completed during the contest is judged. Relevant prior development must be disclosed. The FAQ says private GitHub repositories are accepted if `hackathon@colosseum.com` receives review access. It asks for a **2–3 minute presentation** and a **product demo of at most 3 minutes**, plus product, team, integration and go-to-market information. [Colosseum FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)
- The official rules require submitted content to be in **English** and list functionality, potential impact, novelty, UX, open source and business plan as judging criteria. [Official rules, sections 8 and 12](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)
- The official site lists a **$30,000 grand prize**, twenty further **$15,000** project awards, and a **$100,000 Solana track split across ten projects**. The rules state that the Solana track is for products integrating Solana. Accelerator admission and the advertised **$250,000 pre-seed funding** are a separate selection and investment opportunity, not a cash hackathon prize. [Event page](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair), [official rules, section 14](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf), [FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)

## Meteora DBC side track

- The [Meteora listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/meteora-dbc) shows **20,000 USDC total**: 10,000 / 5,000 / 3,000 / 1,500 / 500 USDC. Its page data gives the closing timestamp **2026-10-13 06:59 UTC**, equivalent to **2026-10-12 23:59 PDT / 2026-10-13 13:59 ICT**. The visible page also gives a sponsor-scheduled winner announcement of October 31.
- The brief focuses on innovative assets and launch flows using **DBC and DAMM v2**. Judging criteria are depth of Meteora integration, technical execution, originality and taste, impact potential, and traction/volume. It **prefers** mainnet projects with active users; the text does not make mainnet an explicit eligibility requirement. It requests read access for GitHub user `dannxbt` when a project is closed source. [Meteora listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/meteora-dbc)
- The listing mentions possible **discretionary infrastructure grants** for selected AI or RWA builders. Those are not guaranteed prizes or operating funds. [Meteora listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/meteora-dbc)

## Solami live-data side track

- The [Solami listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-something-live-on-solana-data) gives the same **2026-10-13 06:59 UTC / 13:59 ICT** closing timestamp in its page data. Its core requirement is that at least one Solami product is a meaningful data path, not a token call. Listed options include RPC/WebSocket, Yellowstone gRPC, Mirage WebSocket, Blur, Data API, webhooks and Beam.
- Submission requires a **public repository** with a runnable README and environment/key setup, and a **2–3 minute demo running against live Solana mainnet**. The listing says a submission that does not run live is not judged. A private repo, replay stream or devnet demonstration therefore does not satisfy this track. [Solami listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-something-live-on-solana-data)
- The listing advertises a seven-day Pro signup offer. This is a service offer, not a cash award; any use, price and limits must be rechecked before relying on it. [Solami listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-something-live-on-solana-data)

Its text requires: submit information and join the linked Telegram group; meet Colosseum eligibility; submit on **both Colosseum and Superteam Earn**; register with **Vietnam** as base country; and pitch at its **October 4, 2026 Demo Day**. The page says an **online pitch is accepted**, but gives no pitch time or timezone. The team must verify the schedule directly. The Vietnam base-country and individual eligibility facts have not been established here.
- The listing describes a **$10,000 prize pool** and additional infrastructure support. Its rendered prize card labels the pool **USDG**, while the body labels individual rewards **USDC**. Treat the payment denomination as unresolved until the organizer clarifies it. The listing also mentions **$2,000 in additional Solami prizes** for top teams using Solami; this is a distinct sponsor award, not part of a guaranteed total. [Vietnam listing](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/colosseum-crypto-worlds-fair-hackathon-superteam-vietnam-track)
